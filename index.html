Media Manager

Import, browse, and back up camera footage.

v0.5.5

How it works

Media Manager is designed around a local-first workflow that keeps your camera free and your footage safe:

  1. Import to your Mac — native USB transfer is fast, so the camera is free to use again in seconds.
  2. Back up to Google Drive — upload runs in the background from your Mac; no need to keep the camera connected.
  3. Clean up — delete the local copy once the upload finishes.

Why not upload directly from the camera? Large video files can take a long time to transfer over the network, tying up your device. A cable connection can also drop mid-transfer. By importing locally first, you get a reliable copy right away and the camera back in your hands.

Resilient imports. If the app is closed or crashes mid-import, it remembers exactly which files were completed and which weren't. On the next launch, interrupted files are clearly flagged so you can re-select and resume without guessing where you left off.

Install

  1. Download the zip for your Mac: Apple Silicon (arm64)  |  Intel (x64)
  2. Unzip and move Media Manager.app to your Applications folder.
  3. Open Terminal and run:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Media\ Manager.app
    This clears the quarantine flag since the app is not notarized.
  4. Open Media Manager from Applications.

Supported devices

  • DJI Osmo Nano – Import videos and photos from the camera SD card (/Volumes/OsmoNano)
  • Insta360 GO 3S – Import videos from the action camera (/Volumes/GO 3S)

Google Drive backup

Media Manager can upload your imported footage to Google Drive for off-site backup. You choose the destination folder — nothing is hardcoded.

  1. Open your Google Drive and create (or pick) a folder for your footage.
  2. Copy the folder URL or ID from the browser address bar.
  3. In Media Manager, paste it into the Google Drive folder field on the Library page.
  4. Connect your Google account via OAuth2, then use Upload to Drive to back up selected files.

Requires OAuth2 credentials (credentials.json). Uploads are resumable — interrupted transfers pick up where they left off.

Google Drive upload is optional and requires OAuth2 credentials.
